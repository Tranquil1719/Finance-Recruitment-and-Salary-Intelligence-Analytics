# Finance, Recruitment & Salary Intelligence Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes finance and recruitment data to uncover insights into hiring trends, salary patterns, applicant activity, and recruitment performance.

The analysis was developed using Power BI to transform raw data into an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Analyze recruitment and hiring trends
- Understand salary patterns across different roles
- Identify factors associated with salary differences
- Analyze applicant and hiring activity
- Evaluate recruitment performance
- Present key findings through an interactive dashboard

## 💡 Key Insights

* Strong demand for finance talent: The dataset records 2,312 job postings across 2,192 hiring companies, attracting approximately 468K applicants, with an average of 202 applicants per job. This indicates a highly competitive finance recruitment market.
* Balanced employment and work arrangements: Recruitment is relatively evenly distributed across full-time, contract, and internship roles, while onsite, hybrid, and remote opportunities also show a fairly balanced distribution. This suggests that finance employers are offering diverse employment and work-model options.
* Certification is highly valued: 81.14% of job postings require professional certification, highlighting the importance of industry-recognized qualifications in finance recruitment. ACCA has the highest number of postings among the certifications shown.
* SQL leads in technical skill demand: Among the finance tools analyzed, SQL has the highest number of job postings (505), followed by SAP (472), Power BI (470), Tableau (458), and Excel (407). This shows that employers increasingly value a combination of financial knowledge and technical/data skills.
* Salary levels remain relatively consistent across experience levels: The average salary midpoint is approximately $77.03K, with senior-level roles averaging about $77.5K, mid-level roles $76.9K, and entry-level roles $76.7K. The relatively narrow gap suggests that salary differences across experience levels are not particularly large within this dataset.
* Recruitment activity is substantial but conversion remains low: The dashboard records approximately 240K interviews and 13K hires, producing a 2.70% hiring success rate. With an average time-to-hire of 32.89 days, the figures point to a competitive recruitment process with a relatively small proportion of candidates progressing to successful hires.
* Audit and corporate finance show strong hiring activity: Audit leads department-level hiring with 324 postings, followed by Corporate Finance (318) and Business Intelligence (285). This indicates strong demand for both traditional finance functions and data-oriented finance roles.
* Recruitment performance remains positive: The average company rating stands at 4.00, while hiring activity is spread across industries, with manufacturing showing the highest hiring activity among the industries displayed.

Overall Key Takeaway

The finance job market is highly competitive and increasingly skills-driven, with strong demand for certified professionals who combine traditional finance expertise with technical capabilities such as SQL, SAP, Power BI, and Tableau. Despite substantial recruitment activity, the relatively low hiring success rate highlights the competitive nature of securing finance roles.
